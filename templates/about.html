About Amination eSports

Amination eSports is a South African esports tournament platform built to organize competitive mobile football gaming tournaments and grow a structured esports community.

FROM IMAGINATION TO REALITY

What is Amination eSports?

Amination eSports is part of the wider Amination brand and focuses on competitive gaming, tournaments and community.

The platform is being built as a real long-term esports system rather than simply a place to display tournament information. Its purpose is to provide a structured home for players, competitions, matches and esports experiences.

Built in South Africa

Amination eSports was founded in South Africa with the goal of building a recognizable esports platform and community from the ground up.

The platform begins with mobile football gaming and is designed with a broader future for competitive gaming in mind.
